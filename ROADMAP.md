ElevateLabs Creatives Roadmap
=============================

## Goals

Studio

- Produce more branded short films and social-first video series.
- Launch a creator-partner program so we can work with and employ more creators.

Services

- Offer subscription creative retainers for growing brands.
- Expand AI-assisted production to deliver faster without losing craft.

Website

- Add case-study pages and a blog.
- Add direct form submissions.
- Improve SEO with pre-rendered pages.

Community

- Host workshops and meetups for local creators.
- Publish free templates and guides.
